## 📊 Portfolio projects

Each project starts from a business question, uses public data, and ends with recommendations, a cost or impact estimate, and an honest list of limitations.

| Project | Question | Highlights |
|---|---|---|
| ⛽ [**What Drives U.S. Gasoline Prices?**](https://github.com/jobayerdu00/oil-gas-price-drivers) | When crude oil moves, what happens at the pump, and how fast? | 21 years of EIA/FRED data · crude explains 77% of pump prices · +$1/bbl ≈ +2.6¢/gal within 2 months · [live scenario tool](https://jobayerdu00.github.io/oil-gas-price-drivers/) |
| 🏥 [**Predicting Hospital Readmissions**](https://github.com/jobayerdu00/hospital-readmission-prediction) | Which diabetic patients will be readmitted within 30 days, and is a follow-up program worth it? | 99K hospital stays · patient-level validation · $2.84 return per $1 for targeted follow-up · fairness audit by race, sex and age |
| 🛒 [**Retail Sales Forecasting**](https://github.com/jobayerdu00/retail-sales-forecasting) | How much will each category sell next year, for inventory and staffing? | Back-tested 4 methods incl. Holt-Winters · quarterly error ~10% · 2018 forecast with 80% ranges · discounts above 20% lose money |
| 💳 [**Credit Card Fraud Detection**](https://github.com/jobayerdu00/credit-card-fraud-detection) | Which transactions should be held for review to minimize total losses? | 284K transactions, 0.17% fraud · time-based split · cost-based alert thresholds cut losses 50%, amount-aware rule 60% |
| 🚚 [**Supply Chain Delivery Risk**](https://github.com/jobayerdu00/supply-chain-delivery-risk) | Why do 57% of orders arrive late, and what should change? | Root cause is unrealistic shipping promises, not geography · data-leakage check · fixes cut late orders from 57% to 16% |
